# Knowledge, composed

An editorial field guide to memory, with a mosaic of source fragments that becomes a readable evidence composition. The warm palette and new typography implement the explicit landing-page brief; existing repository brand assets remain intact.

Palette: paper #f5f2e9, ink #252721, vermilion #b93822, warm white #fffdf7, stone #e5e1d6, secondary ink #626459. Newsreader 400 is the display voice; Hanken Grotesk variable is the reading and interface face. Fonts are local WOFF2, Latin only.

Composition: a compact masthead, asymmetrical hero with an oversized serif statement on the left and a fitted mosaic of evidence on the right. A quiet product strip leads to a dark, spacious interactive workbench. Three library objects in an open diagram bring the page back to paper. A developer example and three linear capability rows lead to a typographic closing invitation.

The hero and walkthrough are related compositions, not decorative graphs. Source fragments, visible dates, an explicit relation and the query converge into inspectable evidence. No answer generation or temporal arbitration is implied. No auto-running effects; the only motion follows deliberate interaction.

Critique before implementation: the brief's warm editorial palette could read as a generic brochure. The defining object will instead be a custom interlocking mosaic with typographic dates and source annotations, repeated only where it explains retrieval. Avoid a grid of feature cards, label proliferation, decorative counters and chart-like performance claims.

Mobile: keep the headline, description and main action first; stack the mosaic and library roles deliberately. Demo controls become a two-by-two step selector, with persistent explanatory copy and a source sheet below.
